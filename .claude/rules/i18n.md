# App text and translation

- The app's own text is translated (`docs/i18n.md`): no English in a
  template, handler message or script. Every text is a key in `i18n/en.yaml`
  and in every other `i18n/<code>.yaml` (`cargo test i18n` fails on a
  missing key), read with `crate::t("area.thing")` / `t1` / `tn` in Rust and
  templates and `t('area.js.thing')` in scripts. Courses are not translated
  here (a translated copy is, #630).
- Dev-mode pages are English only: the `/dev` pages (`templates/dev_*.html`)
  and `{% if dev %}` panels may write their text in the template, with no
  `i18n` keys (#903).
