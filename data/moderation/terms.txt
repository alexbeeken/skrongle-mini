# Terms a made course may not contain (#1033, src/course_flags/terms.rs).
#
# A course with one of these is flagged: nobody can start it or share it
# until its owner takes the word out. Built-in courses are not checked.
#
# What belongs here: words that are never fine in a language course, in any
# language the app teaches. Slurs and hate phrases, nothing else. Leave out
# words that are ordinary in another language ("negro" is "black" in
# Spanish, "retard" is "late" in French, "coger" is everyday Spanish) and
# words with a common harmless meaning ("chink in the armour"). A superuser
# can clear a course the list flags by mistake.
#
# One term per line; a line starting with # is a comment. A term can be
# several words. Matching is whole word, case-insensitive and ignores
# accents, so list each plural or other form you mean. The test
# `course_flags::tests::the_built_in_courses_have_no_listed_term` keeps
# the list from flagging the built-in courses.
